## Unit One: Earthquakes
- **Coding concepts:** Introduced to foundational coding concepts, including variables, data types, loops, and debugging common errors using Python.
- **Scientific concepts:** Learned the fundamentals of earthquakes, seismic waves and models, different lava types, tectonic plate speeds, Earth’s internal layers, and the various types of tectonic plate boundaries.
**Unit project:** Developed a map of recent earthquakes in Central America. Presented research on current earthquake activity and proposed plans to improve earthquake damage prevention and preparedness.
